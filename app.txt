App Name: AviGarden
Version: 1.0.0
Platform: Android / iOS
Category: Birdwatching / Nature
Target Market: Global
Price Model: Free + $2.99/month Pro

Description:
A local geo-tracking log for backyard birders to record sightings, log active feeder food levels, and get local migration alerts when target species are near.

Key Features:
- Bird sighting log with species, time, GPS location
- Photo capture per sighting directly in app
- Feeder management: log food type, fill level, last refill date
- Local migration alerts via eBird API for your zip code
- Life list: track all unique species ever spotted
- Species identifier: upload photo → AI suggests likely species
- Seasonal calendar showing which birds are expected in your area
- Offline species database (no internet needed for logging)
- Share sightings with local birdwatching communities

Target Audience: Backyard birdwatchers, nature enthusiasts, wildlife hobbyists
Competitors: Merlin Bird ID, eBird, iNaturalist
Unique Value: Combines feeder management + migration alerts + sighting log in one app
